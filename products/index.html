How we vet

A product earns a place here three ways.

Anything that fails one of these does not reach your formulary through us, however well it sells elsewhere.

01 · Clinical

The evidence is what it is

We read the actual studies, not the brochure. Where a platform rests on case series and posters rather than randomised trials, the product page says so — because an auditor will find out either way.

02 · Coverage

The code has to be real

Every platform carries a verified HCPCS or CPT pathway and a documentation set built against it. Payment amounts are set by CMS and the payer, and we confirm current status per case rather than printing a number.

03 · Operational

Your staff has to be able to run it

Chair-side handling, storage, in-service burden, and reorder friction all count. A product that needs a freezer, a thaw cycle, and a second pair of hands is a product that quietly stops being used in month three.
