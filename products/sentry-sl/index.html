Single-layer amnion · 361 HCT/P · HCPCS Q4348

Sentry™ SL — the single-layer amnion allograft.

Positioned for shallow-to-moderate wound beds.

Sentry™ Placental-Derived Allograft, from Sequence LifeScience, Inc. of San Antonio, TX, is comprised of a single layer of amniotic membrane — processed to serve as a barrier or wound cover for acute and chronic wounds. The amnion is a thin, highly compact extracellular matrix whose elasticity lets it conform closely to the wound surface. Albacete MedDev distributes Sentry™ and supports the coding and documentation around it.

The material

One membrane, processed to stay close to native.

Sequence LifeScience describes placental tissue as “a pristine newly formed extracellular matrix” that “represents an untainted extracellular matrix…essentially a neomatrix.” Sentry™ takes the amnion — the fetus-facing layer of that tissue — and keeps it as one intact membrane.

Amnion ECM

Thin, highly compact matrix

The amnion layer is constituted of collagen fibers — predominantly Types I, III, IV and V — along with laminin, fibronectin, proteoglycans and hyaluronic acid. A dense extracellular scaffold in a low-profile membrane.

Conformability

Follows the wound surface

The manufacturer’s design case, verbatim: “The elasticity and durability of the amnion layer allows it to conform closely to wound surfaces while providing a protective barrier.” One layer, low bulk, close contact — the argument for shallow-to-moderate wound beds.

Processing

Minimally manipulated, dehydrated

Sentry™ is processed with Sequence LifeScience’s proprietary tissue technique, dehydrated, and verified terminally sterilized. Per the manufacturer, finished allografts retain the structural and functional characteristics of the starting placental membrane.

Clinical fit

Where a single layer earns its place.

Per its Instructions for Use, Sentry™ Placental-Derived Allograft (361 HCT/P) is “intended as a natural biologic wound covering or skin substitute for cutaneous wounds,” applied by qualified health care professionals in a physician office, outpatient, or inpatient setting.

Sentry™ or Palisade™?

Sequence LifeScience builds both from placental tissue. The difference is construction, and it maps to the wound in front of you.

  • Sentry™ is the single-layer graft: amnion only, thin and compact. The manufacturer positions it “particularly when applied to shallow-to-moderate wound beds,” where elasticity and close conformity to the wound surface matter most.
  • Palisade™ is the dual-membrane graft — amnion plus chorion — billed under its own HCPCS code, Q4350.
  • Both are minimally manipulated placental allografts for acute and chronic wounds, and both are distributed by Albacete MedDev. Our clinical science team can walk your protocol through the choice.
Evidence & tissue safety

Graded honestly, sourced carefully.

We state evidence at its actual weight. Here is what stands behind Sentry™ today — and what the published materials do not claim.

Literature

Tissue-science reviews, not Sentry™ trials

The manufacturer’s brochure cites two peer-reviewed reviews — Niknejad et al., Eur Cell Mater 2008;15:88–99, and Roy et al., BioMed Research International 2022:1–26 — on the properties of amniotic membrane and placental tissues as biomaterials generally. They support the mechanism rationale for placental ECM. They are not clinical trials of Sentry™, and we will not present them as outcome evidence.

Donor program

Consented donors, accredited recovery

Donated tissue comes from fully consented mothers undergoing full-term c-sections, obtained in partnership with FDA-regulated and accredited recovery organizations. Each donor is screened according to U.S. Food and Drug Administration requirements and American Association of Tissue Banks standards.

Sterility

Validated terminal sterilization

Finished Sentry™ allografts undergo a validated terminal sterilization process. Screening details are documented in the product package insert and Instructions for Use — ask us for a copy.

Sizing & coding

Four sizes, one code.

Sentry™ is reported under HCPCS Level II code Q4348, defined per square centimeter. Match the graft to the wound; the code follows the area.

Coding, stated exactly

The published HCPCS descriptor for Q4348 reads “Sentry SL matrix, per square centimeter” — which is why our catalog lists the product as Sentry SL. Sequence LifeScience’s own ordering information reads “Sentry™ Matrix, per square centimeter.” Same graft, same code.

  • Q4348 describes billing, not coverage. Verify each payer’s policy before scheduling application.
  • Four sizes from 4 to 32 sq cm let you match graft area to measured wound area — and document why.
  • Albacete MedDev provides coding and documentation support: wound measurement records, graft-size justification, and application notes.
FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

Is Sentry™ FDA approved or FDA cleared? +
Neither — and no product in this class is. Sentry™ is a human cell and tissue product (361 HCT/P) regulated solely under Section 361 of the Public Health Service Act and FDA’s HCT/P regulation, 21 CFR Part 1271 — a pathway that does not involve premarket approval or clearance. What stands behind it instead: donor screening to FDA requirements and American Association of Tissue Banks standards, tissue recovery through FDA-regulated and accredited organizations, and a validated terminal sterilization process.
Why does the catalog say “Sentry SL” when the brochure says “Sentry™ Placental-Derived Allograft”? +
Both name the same graft. The manufacturer’s product name is Sentry™ Placental-Derived Allograft. The official HCPCS Level II descriptor for Q4348 reads “Sentry SL matrix, per square centimeter,” and we list products the way the code book does. The manufacturer’s ordering information states the code as “Sentry™ Matrix, per square centimeter.”
How is Sentry™ different from Palisade™? +
Construction. Sentry™ is a single layer of amnion — thin, compact, and positioned by the manufacturer particularly for shallow-to-moderate wound beds, where its elasticity lets it conform closely to the wound surface. Palisade™ is the dual-membrane allograft (amnion plus chorion) reported under HCPCS Q4350. Same manufacturer; each product’s own documentation describes its donor screening and validated terminal sterilization.
Is there clinical trial data on Sentry™? +
Not in the manufacturer materials we distribute. The brochure cites two peer-reviewed tissue-science reviews on placental membranes as biomaterials (Niknejad 2008; Roy 2022) — mechanism literature, not Sentry™ outcome trials. If your program needs comparative evidence for a specific wound type, our clinical science team will tell you plainly what exists and what does not.
How is Sentry™ billed, and will it be covered? +
The code is HCPCS Level II Q4348, defined per square centimeter; the four sizes run 4 to 32 sq cm. Coverage is each payer’s decision, and we do not promise it. We support the parts you control: coding accuracy, wound documentation, and graft-size justification — payer by payer.
