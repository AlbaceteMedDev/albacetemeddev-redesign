Before you order

Who can order. We accept requests only from licensed prescribers and their practices. A 503A pharmacy compounds for an identified patient under a patient-specific prescription; 503A preparations cannot be supplied as office stock. A 503B outsourcing facility may supply office-use stock only where federal and state law allow, and only the outsourcing facility may sell or transfer that stock — it may not be resold or passed on to another practice.

Not FDA-approved. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. FDA does not review them for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they reach patients. FDA has not determined that any compounded preparation is equivalent to, or interchangeable with, an FDA-approved drug.

Active ingredients of FDA-approved drugs. Federal law bars a 503A pharmacy from compounding, regularly or in inordinate amounts, a drug that is essentially a copy of a commercially available FDA-approved drug, and separately bars a 503B outsourcing facility from compounding drugs that are essentially copies of approved drugs. A preparation is not considered a copy when the prescriber determines, as a clinical judgment for an individual patient, that a specific change (for example, leaving out an ingredient the patient cannot tolerate) produces a significant difference for that patient; FDA guidance asks that the change and the difference be noted on the prescription. We list these substances only for patient-specific 503A prescriptions that carry that note. A 503B outsourcing facility may compound from a bulk drug substance only if the substance is on FDA's 503B bulks list (or in Category 1 under FDA's 503B interim policy) or the drug is on FDA's drug shortage list when it is compounded, distributed, and dispensed. A 503A pharmacy may compound semaglutide or tirzepatide only from the form that is the active ingredient of the approved drugs; salt forms (for example, semaglutide sodium or semaglutide acetate) do not qualify.

Status chips. Each entry shows our reading of its federal status; the legend under the filters defines every chip. "Bulks Category 1" means FDA has placed the substance in Category 1 under its interim policy for the 503A bulks list, the 503B bulks list, or both — substances FDA is still evaluating. FDA has said it does not intend to take action against compounding from them while that evaluation continues, provided every other legal condition is met. It is not FDA approval and not a finding of safety or effectiveness. State law may be stricter than federal policy. Entries marked "Not available to order" are listed for reference only.

Research. Catalog entries do not summarize research. Our Evidence page grades published research on each substance (often laboratory or animal studies), not research on any compounded preparation. We make no claim that any preparation is safe or effective for any use, and we do not provide dosing guidance.

Who compounds and dispenses. Albacete MedDev is not a pharmacy or an outsourcing facility. It does not compound, dispense, or prescribe, and it does not practice medicine or direct prescribing decisions. Each order is filled by the supplier for the channel shown on the entry, not by Albacete MedDev. Before you order, ask us for the supplier's legal name, its license, and, for a 503B outsourcing facility, its FDA registration. You are free to use any appropriately licensed pharmacy; ordering through these channels is never required.

Prescriber responsibility. The prescriber decides whether a compounded preparation is appropriate for a patient instead of an FDA-approved alternative, and is responsible for licensure and a valid practitioner–patient relationship under the law of the patient's state, informed consent (including that the preparation is not FDA-approved), the choice of dose and route, monitoring, and, for active ingredients of FDA-approved drugs, the significant-difference determination noted on the prescription.

State availability. Whether a preparation can be dispensed depends on the dispensing entity's licensure in the state where the preparation will be delivered and on that state's law. Not every preparation is available in every state.

Adverse events and quality concerns. Report suspected adverse events and product-quality problems to the dispensing pharmacy or outsourcing facility and to FDA MedWatch at www.fda.gov/medwatch or 1-800-FDA-1088. To tell us about a quality concern, call 551-497-3428 — please do not email patient details.

Privacy. Do not send patient names or any other patient health information by email.

This page is not medical advice. Pricing information is provided on request to licensed prescribers and their practices; requesting pricing creates no obligation. Before any order, we will tell you which entity receives the prescription or order, invoices you, and ships the preparation.
