The Problem

Review changes in products, services, and payer requirements.

Product options, payer policies, and service requirements change over time. A structured review can identify where existing processes, documentation, or product choices merit reassessment.

Referral analysis can help a practice understand local referral patterns, service demand, and opportunities to develop professional relationships.

How It Works

Analyze the practice. Evaluate options. Plan implementation.

A structured review of your product portfolio, service activity, and workflows, followed by a practical implementation plan.

Step 01

Portfolio analysis

Review product utilization, rendered services, encounter-level financial information, and existing workflows to establish a baseline.

  • Full product-line audit across your current formulary
  • Service activity by location and frequency
  • Encounter-level revenue & margin analysis
  • Existing workflow & EMR integration review
Step 02

Opportunity discovery

Use available market and practice data to review product options, service activity, payer coverage, and referral patterns. Evaluate potential changes in the context of clinical appropriateness and the practice’s capabilities.

  • Product options, evidence, and costs
  • Clinical service development opportunities
  • Product and service workflow assessment
  • Payer coverage and documentation review
  • Local referral-pattern analysis
Step 03

Implementation planning

Define the process changes, training, documentation, and reporting needed for implementation. Review responsibilities and progress with practice leadership.

  • Product implementation plan
  • Service-line integration tailored to your workflow
  • Portal-based revenue tracking & reconciliation
  • Ongoing reviews at agreed intervals
Money on the table

Areas included in the revenue cycle review.

The review considers the relationship between services delivered, documentation, coding, product utilization, payer requirements, and referral activity.

Service development

Evaluate additional services in relation to patient needs, clinical capabilities, staffing, and applicable requirements.

Product selection and costs

Review product options, costs, clinical information, and applicable coverage requirements with your practice.

Product and service workflows

Review how products and rendered services are used together, including documentation, coding, and operational requirements.

Supply utilization and billing

Review supplies used at the point of care and assess how they are documented and billed under applicable payer rules.

Encounter coding

Review whether selected codes accurately reflect the documented services and applicable coding requirements.

Referral patterns

Assess local referral activity, service demand, and opportunities to develop professional relationships.

Payer coverage

Review relevant payer policies and the documentation associated with the services your practice provides.

Clinical documentation

Identify documentation gaps and develop guidance or templates to support accurate recording of the services delivered.
