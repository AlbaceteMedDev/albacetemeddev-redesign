Advisory services

Areas of advisory support.

Work with practice leadership to review coding, documentation, operational workflows, product selection, and payer requirements.

Coding strategy

Per-product, per-payer CPT and HCPCS strategy tuned to your specialty. Modifier sequencing, unit billing, and revenue capture reviewed at the claim level.

Denial root-cause analysis

Denials triaged to the underlying driver — coding, documentation, prior-auth, benefit verification, or payer policy shift — with remediation plans attached.

Operations & workflow

Review patient flow, scheduling, staff responsibilities, and benefit-verification workflows to identify areas for improvement.

Formulary design

Evaluate the product portfolio in relation to your patient population, clinical focus, available evidence, and payer requirements.

Payer coverage & market access

Coverage posture across Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial — plus reimbursement pathway design for new products, service lines, and indications.

Compliance & audit readiness

Review documentation standards, compliance processes, and charts in preparation for internal or external review.
