Blood-based & non-sheet skin substitutes

ActiGraft+

RedDress Ltd. · Point-of-care autologous whole blood clot (AWBC) tissue formed chairside from the patient's own peripheral blood using the RD-1/RD-2 (ActiGraft/ActiGraft PRO) clotting system.

A

One product-specific multicenter RCT (n=119) met its primary endpoint at 12 weeks, and a 2026 meta-analysis of six RCTs of autologous blood-derived products (n=629) found the ActiGraft clot the best-performing modality. More than twenty further studies cover diabetic foot, venous, pressure, surgical, pilonidal and fistula wounds.
