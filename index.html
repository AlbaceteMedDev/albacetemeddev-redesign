A clinical starting point

Start with the questions that matter.

A product name is only the beginning. We bring the clinical information and practical considerations into the same conversation.

  1. 01

    What is the product designed to do?

    Review the product’s composition, intended use, instructions, and application requirements before considering its fit.

  2. 02

    What does the evidence actually show?

    Look at study design, patient population, outcomes, and limitations. Distinguish evidence for a specific product from research on its broader class.

  3. 03

    How would it fit into your practice?

    Discuss training, ordering, documentation, and reimbursement questions alongside your existing care pathway.
