Common questions

Ovena Health, answered.

How is Ovena Health related to Albacete MedDev? +
Ovena Health is the direct-to-patient brand in the Albacete MedDev family. Albacete MedDev supplies and supports clinical practices; Ovena carries the same clinical standards to patients at home — through ovenahealth.com and the Amazon Brand Store.
What should patients buy on Amazon vs. ovenahealth.com? +
Amazon carries the hydrocolloid rolls, compression socks (M/L/XL) and the sock aid with Prime convenience. The collagen line — dressings, powder, kits — plus saline and full size runs sell on ovenahealth.com, which also offers free shipping over $75 and FSA/HSA checkout.
Are Ovena products actually clinical-grade? +
The collagen dressing line is FDA 510(k)-cleared and the brand states ISO 13485 manufacturing with physician review across the catalog — “FDA 510(k)-cleared where applicable, honest labels.” Compression is medical-grade 20-30 mmHg graduated knee-high.
Can a practice or agency buy at volume? +
Yes — Ovena runs a bulk-orders program for facilities and home-health agencies (“from one box to a thousand”), and Albacete MedDev can fold Ovena supply into a broader practice partnership. Talk to us about facility pricing.
