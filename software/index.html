Why Now

A ruler, a tracing, and a photograph — and an auditor who isn’t convinced.

Documentation stopped being a clinical nicety. It is a revenue driver and an audit requirement. Most practices still measure wounds with a disposable ruler, a tracing, and a photograph — and that record is subjective, inconsistent between providers, and hard to defend when a reviewer asks how the number was derived.

The record today

Subjective by construction

Ruler-and-tracing measurement varies by who held the ruler and on what day. Two clinicians measuring the same wound produce two different numbers — and neither can prove which one was right.

The exposure

Substantiating the billed area

On review, the units billed have to be supported by what is in the chart — the measured area, the method behind it, and who recorded it. The product was applied. The record has to be able to carry it.

What changes

Same capture, same number

The same capture and the same confirmed boundary produce the same measurement every time, by any clinician, on any day — and the boundary the clinician confirmed is stored with the measurement. Where two clinicians confirm different boundaries, the areas will differ; the confirmed outline is in the report, so the difference is visible rather than hidden.

This is the same problem our audit-defense and revenue-cycle teams work every week. The documentation that defends a skin-substitute claim under a UPIC or ADR review is, in practice, a defensible measurement of the wound on the date of service. StrataMetric produces exactly that record.

Why “instrument” is the operative word

A camera app takes a picture and estimates. An instrument declares its method, carries its uncertainty, and produces the same answer twice. The difference shows up the day an auditor asks how the number was derived.

  • Per-region noiseSensor noise is modeled per region, not globally — a shadowed undermined edge is not given the same confidence as flat peri-wound skin.
  • Uncertainty carried throughMonte Carlo sampling propagates uncertainty into every derived measurement. Nothing is reported as a bare number with no error behind it.
  • Clinician in the loopThe segmentation model proposes the wound outline. The clinician reviews and can refine it before any measurement is computed.
  • Volume that is actually integratedMost tools multiply length × width × depth and call it volume. This engine integrates the depth field across the whole wound bed — so an undermined or irregular wound reports the volume it actually has.
  • Depth from a fitted planeDepth is measured from a least-squares-fitted reference plane of surrounding healthy skin down to the wound floor — not from an eyeballed rim.
  • Method on the pageMethodology is disclosed in every measurement report, alongside algorithm versions and content-hashed inputs.

How It Works

Scan to structured report, in five stages.

Each stage is versioned, tested, and recorded in the report the clinician signs. Nothing in the chain is a black box the practice cannot describe to a reviewer.

01 · Capture

iPhone — ARKit + LiDAR

A clinician positions an iPhone over the wound. The app captures a burst of 60 LiDAR depth frames in four seconds. No cart, no tripod, no dedicated capital equipment.

02 · Reconstruct

Uncertainty-aware sensor fusion

Heteroscedastic Gaussian-process fusion with Monte Carlo bounds. Noise is modeled per region rather than globally, and that uncertainty is carried through every downstream measurement.

03 · Segment

ML boundary detection

A segmentation model — SAM, the Segment Anything Model — proposes the wound outline from the captured imagery. The clinician reviews and can refine it before measurements are computed — the boundary is confirmed, never assumed.

04 · Measure

3D geometry engine

Depth is measured from a least-squares-fitted reference plane of surrounding healthy skin down to the wound floor. Wound bed area integrates over the actual traced boundary, not a bounding rectangle.

05 · Report

Diagrams + plain language

A structured measurement report with diagrams, a plain-language narrative, disclosed methodology, and full provenance — ready for the chart and ready for a reviewer.

What the geometry engine computes

The five reported measurements, each derived a specific way and each traceable back to the frames it came from — and the integrated volume behind them.

  • Length & widthMajor and minor axes computed from point-cloud geometry — not the longest ruler line a clinician happened to choose.
  • True depthPlane-fitted — a least-squares reference plane of surrounding healthy skin, measured down to the wound floor.
  • Wound bed areaA boundary integral over the actual traced outline. Non-rectangular wounds report non-rectangular area.
  • Peri-wound areaMeasured in the same scan, on the same reference plane — not estimated separately from a second photograph.
  • VolumeThe depth field is integrated across the whole wound bed, so undermined and irregular geometry is represented rather than approximated by length × width × depth.

Tamper-evident provenance

Every measurement stores content-hashed inputs, the algorithm versions used — confidence weights, force-correction tables, ML weights — and a hash chain over inputs, algorithm versions and outputs. That chain is exported with the report, so a reviewer can be told precisely how a number on the chart was produced and can confirm that the record matches what was signed.

How the engine is validated

176+ automated engine tests run in continuous integration across six validation layers. Nothing ships on a demo that looked good once.

  • Synthetic meshesGenerated geometry with known ground truth — the engine’s answer is checked against the number the mesh was built to have.
  • Property-based invariantsProperties that must hold for any valid input, tested across generated cases rather than a handful of fixtures.
  • Performance benchmarksScan-to-report latency held to the sub-five-minute target as the pipeline changes.
  • Physical phantom calibrationPhantoms of known dimension, used to calibrate and check the instrument against physical reality.
  • Clinical comparison harnessA harness for comparing engine output against clinical reference measurement. A harness is an evaluation apparatus — not, by itself, a published clinical study.
  • Regulatory conformance checksAutomated checks tied to the regulatory traceability matrix, so conformance is verified on every build.

100% of reports carry full provenance and methodology. Not a sample. Not on request. Every report, by construction — because a report that cannot explain itself is not evidence.

No accuracy or precision figure is published on this page. The engine is checked against synthetic meshes with known ground truth and against physical phantoms of known dimension on every build, but a precision number is only meaningful with its method, sample size, reference standard and scan distance attached — until that test report can be published alongside it, no such figure is claimed here and none should be inferred.
