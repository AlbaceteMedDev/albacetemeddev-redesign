Why it exists

Length × width × depth cannot see the wound.

Wound beds are never uniform. Undermining, tunneling and uneven granulation all change how much volume is really there — and ruler-and-photo documentation is increasingly inadequate under Medicare audit. AI Wound Scan integrates the depth field across the wound’s own topography, so an undermined or irregular wound reports what it really holds.

01 · Capture

Four-second capture

A clinician points an iPhone. A LiDAR burst collects 60 depth frames in about four seconds — no rulers, no disposable markers, no probe in the wound bed.

02 · Measure

Volume the wound actually has

The engine integrates the depth field across the reconstructed wound bed rather than approximating with L×W×D. Surface area, depth and perimeter come with it — each with a 95% confidence interval.

03 · Document

Documentation that defends itself

Auto-generated, audit-defensible notes with full provenance and methodology in every report, clinical notes with sign-and-lock, and a tamper-evident audit hash chain.

04 · Claims

Claims & compliance

Documentation-compliance checks against claims requirements, built for the CMS/LCD/NCD context wound programs actually bill in.

05 · Inventory

Graft inventory with UDI

Serial, lot and UDI captured at the point of care, so every graft unit is traceable from manufacturer to application to the claim it was billed on.

06 · Operate

The service line around it

Patient and wound tracking with healing trajectories, reporting and analytics, route planning for mobile providers, and an admin console with a live tamper-evidence check.

Built for the pathway ahead

StrataMetric’s stated regulatory posture, quoted plainly — and its limits, stated just as plainly:

  • Cures Act §3060 CDS exemption — the clinician retains decision authority, no diagnostic claims are made, and methodology is displayed in every report.
  • 510(k)-ready architecture — designed to support a future FDA submission. Not FDA-cleared and not FDA-approved today.
  • HIPAA-grade controls — TOTP MFA, role-based access, tamper-evident audit chain, encryption at rest and in transit. A control posture, not a certification.
  • Six-year retention — object-locked storage aligned to documentation-retention expectations.

Required framing: For clinical decision support only. Not for diagnostic use. Clinician retains decision authority. Methodology disclosed in every measurement report.

Common questions

What to know about AI Wound Scan.

What exactly is StrataMetric, and how does it relate to Albacete MedDev? +
StrataMetric is the software company behind AI Wound Scan; in its own words, “AI Wound Scan is a StrataMetric product, part of the Albacete MedDev ecosystem.” It pairs naturally with the wound-care products and reimbursement services on this site — one partner across products, documentation and software.
Is it FDA cleared? +
No. AI Wound Scan operates today under the 21st Century Cures Act §3060 clinical-decision-support exemption: the clinician retains decision authority and no diagnostic claims are made. Its architecture is described as 510(k)-ready — designed to support a future submission — which is not the same thing as cleared or approved, and this page never treats it as such.
What hardware does it need? +
An iPhone 12 Pro or later — the LiDAR-equipped hardware clinics already carry. Capture runs in a guided SwiftUI app; the portal runs in the browser.
What does it cost? +
Pricing is not public; access is by request while practices onboard. Email gabe@stratametricai.com for an access request or a guided walkthrough.
